# Normal-map lighting study

Use **Test > Labs > Normal-map lighting study**, or `--lab lighting-study`. The lab replaces the current level only after the normal confirmation. Its source and progress are disposable. The registered level can also be opened through Level Select; `properties.surface_study` supplies its library, material, directional light and ambient profile. It does not rely on a launcher-only setup script.

The four cards use one surface renderer: unlit albedo, flat-normal lighting, normal-map lighting and transformed world normals. Choose an eligible material from the registered library. Preview controls change copies; authored changes belong to F10's existing Terrain, Lights, Lighting and Shader Profiles documents and take effect in a fresh test/session. **Ctrl+F9** hides or restores the study; F9 alone retains its gameplay meaning when the study is hidden. F10 opens the database; F12 opens spatial editing. The world pauses while the study is displayed. Light orbit uses presentation time, not another game calendar.

Normals use +X right, +Y up and +Z toward the viewer; DirectX conversion reverses green. World coordinates have +Y down. Positive UV rotation turns the texture and its normals clockwise; negative UV scales mirror both. Scale magnitude changes tiling. Albedo and light colors are sRGB and are converted to linear space for shading; normal RGB is raw data, independent of alpha and tint. The study preserves separate map dimensions and supports opaque color PNGs within existing limits. Its original calibration images include an asymmetric96x80 checker and128x128 normal maps. The axis map includes transparent pixels to verify that normal data is not premultiplied or alpha-masked. GPU reference samples avoid ambiguous nearest-texel ties.

The first model is adjustable diffuse lighting plus roughness-controlled highlights, ambient light and scalar emission. Light angle points toward the source, clockwise from world right; elevation0 grazes the surface and90 faces the viewer. Results are clamped to the display range. This is a stylized unoccluded surface study, not full PBR. Height, AO, roughness and emission **map sampling**, calendar projection, point/spot placement, shadows, gameplay-light queries remain unbound. Trusted-source profiles are never executed by this adapter. Visual relief does not change collision.

`--lab lighting-check` drives the actual material selector and hide/show shortcut, compares GPU readback with an independent CPU reference, checks state restoration, and exercises owned image reentry and nonfatal shader refusal. It is an in-engine scenario; there is no external test framework. Evidence and disposable workspaces are excluded from public packages.

Calibration normal data stays under `generated/lighting-study/` so it does not enter the albedo texture selector. These are immutable original fixtures, not user-generated outputs or a claim that the companion-map generator is implemented.

The shared lighting function is also bound to real terrain through static profiles. See [Static terrain lighting](TERRAIN_LIGHTING.md); the original comparison cards remain available.
